Categories Miscellaneous

What has always impressed me is Lao Luo's style of doing things and his attitude, especially that saying: Not for winning or losing, I'm just being serious (I think this applies to everyone who writes code). I've also compiled some of Lao Luo's quotes from recent years to share with everyone!
