10 Ways to Boost Web Programmer Motivation

Category Programming Technology

Like other industries, Web design work can actually be rather dull and boring. If we always spend all our time on work and ignore everything else, it will instead affect our passion and motivation.

Passion and motivation are a magical force that can drive us to focus wholeheartedly, work with undivided attention, and face challenges without even a frown. If you want to do well and succeed in this field, then passion and motivation are absolutely indispensable. Or in short, it's like a feedback loop.

No matter how much you love Web design work, it's possible that you're just going through the motions, doing the bare minimum each day and just casually checking your computer like a routine inspection; or you might deeply feel that as long as no one makes you work on websites (or even take a look at one), you'd be fine doing anything else. But because of time constraints or goal setting, you feel like you're being forced to work.

If you've already developed this mindset, then you need to be careful: it's time to stir up your enthusiasm and develop your motivation. This is very important. Only by relying on an unending stream of drive and motivation can we break through the limitations of our abilities, concentrate fully, and make our careers flourish with vitality.

One thing that no school has ever taught you is this: when dealing with clients and career-related issues, there will always be times when you don't want to start designing at all, or even touch a web page. If you don't handle these situations well, it could have a fatal impact on your career.

Therefore, staying motivated is extremely important for every web designer. If we were to rank it, it would definitely be No. 1. Below I'll introduce the 10 ways I, as a Web designer, maintain lasting motivation.

1. Don't Overwork Yourself

No matter what work you do, remember that you're only human, not a robot. No matter the situation, you need rest. Avoiding overwork is the right and effective way to stay motivated. Start with getting enough sleep, manage your workload effectively, and know which clients to take on and which projects to refuse. For the projects you accept, constantly remind yourself of deadlines. It's best to create a corresponding work schedule and then stick to it firmly. This will ensure you have enough time to complete your work gracefully.

Note: Never procrastinate! You should know, "Tomorrow after tomorrow, how many tomorrows are there? If we wait for tomorrow every day, all things will come to naught." Work hard, move toward your goals step by step, undaunted by hardships. Maintain your ambition and refuse procrastination, as this will save you from countless sleepless, day-and-night, overtime, painful and agonizing nights later on.

2. Know When to Give Up

Most people get bored very easily and then start feeling restless when facing work. I'm no exception, and this prevents us from reaching higher levels. However, when a huge website project really hits me like a bomb, my nerves immediately tense up, the motor in my heart starts going "thump thump thump," and a heroic ambition of "daring to compare heights with heaven" fills my heart. The word "give up" had long been kicked to the far corners of the earth by me. That's not good, not good.

Staying motivated means you clearly know when to work, when to go on vacation, or when to take a day off and rest without worrying about work. Knowing when to give up is not the same as quitting. On the contrary, the reason why so many designers and freelancers abandon their careers is that they don't know when to call it quits, and thus fail to manage their time in the most effective way.

Free time means you can rest, have fun, exercise, soothe your mind, and invigorate your body. And if you already have a small family, you especially need to take time out to be with them from time to time. They can even become a source of motivation for you. If you're still single, then you have even more reason to take a break. Otherwise, how would you have time to go on dates, call or text your girlfriend, or even go on miserable blind dates?

Always working like a machine without rest will instead drive your passion and motivation away from you. So, think about it!

3. Refuse All Kinds of Disturbances

This is one of the most important measures, because instant messaging tools like email, QQ, Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and various other instant tools, while providing us convenience, also have a huge drawback: they constantly interrupt our work. Always having to spend a few minutes checking your RSS or glancing at whether there are new emails will take up a large portion of our working time. As one grows, the other shrinks, so the time spent on projects becomes less. Therefore, blocking and limiting these tools is currently the only and best way to stay motivated.

4. Change Your Work Environment

An unchanging work environment can easily make people tired. At times like this, you can try taking your laptop and working in another location, or simply changing your environment is also a good method. You can try working in teahouses, cafés, libraries, and bookstores, which will quickly ignite your passion and soothe your brain. Research has shown that we should go to at least 2-3 different places to work comfortably there and nurture motivation.

5. Enjoy Your Work

Treat work as fun and enjoyment, put a little thought into it, add a little creativity, and make our results more competitive. It's best to get everyone involved, because shared joy is greater than solitary joy. I've found this to be one of the best ways to keep myself motivated.

6. Eat and Drink Well

Besides needing to take intermittent breaks to keep your mind clear, lunch is also very important. It not only provides energy but also gives you a sense of satisfaction. If you're already at the city gates but find it hard to break through the siege and successfully "capture the city," then you might as well go have a snack or lunch first. When you come back and sit in front of the computer again, you'll feel, "Wow, I'm back at full HP! I can continue charging into battle, slaying enemies and conquering cities!" Not just eating, drinking water is also essential, and programmers need to drink water even more. Remember, your energy is beyond your imagination.

7. Vent Stress and Relieve Anxiety

It's not just those with severe anxiety disorders who need to vent stress. Ordinary people, like you and me, can also benefit greatly from relieving anxiety in a timely manner. Suffering under the torment of stress will directly lead to a decrease in motivation. And a simple phrase like "I can do it" will greatly boost morale. To me, this phrase is as magical as a spell in magic. I'm not joking, and I don't tell just anyone.

8. Keep Track of Progress

After working on a project for a while, you often lose track of progress, and at that point you can't help but feel that finishing it is truly a bit out of reach. That's very bad. So, at all times, you should pay attention to the overall progress and give yourself a nudge at the right time to maintain momentum.

9. Attend Seminars Related to Your Work

Seminars are a fantastic environment that can get your passion soaring. I swear, among so many speakers, there will definitely be one who can bring you some new inspiration or a new perspective that makes your eyes light up or leaves you utterly amazed.

You might even find yourself unable to wait to go try these new things right away. Haha.

However, as everyone knows, seminars aren't entirely about learning. Besides learning new things, they also discuss the development of the web and social media, and you might even have the chance to exchange ideas with other Web enthusiasts (possibly over a meal or drinks together). This not only improves your motivation level but also reminds you of why you chose Web design in the first place, and inexplicably gives you a feeling of "wow, my job is truly great."

Sometimes it's our boss who forces us to attend such conferences, but after seeing the benefits above, do you still think they're boring as hell? Cheer up, enjoy it, and treat it as a positive task where you can learn something.

10. Don't Give Up, Every Day Is a New Day

Everyone has times of failure, and everyone has things that discourage them. This is normal; everyone has moments of low mood. But, as the saying goes, "Quitters never win," so never give up easily. Remember, every day is a new day; success or failure depends entirely on how you spend it. Learn from your mistakes, and don't let anything defeat you.

How do you stay motivated?

Everyone is different. All roads lead to Rome, so everyone's way of staying motivated may be different. Why not share with everyone how you deal with this problem.
