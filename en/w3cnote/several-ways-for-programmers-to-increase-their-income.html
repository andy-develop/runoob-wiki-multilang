Several ways for programmers to increase income

Category Programmer Life

In this rapidly developing Internet society, learning how to make money is very important. We're just ordinary people; making money is the top priority for survival in society. This is not a world of lofty elegance, but one where ability creates wealth—especially for programmers. As programmers, we have more ways to earn money. Pay attention, pay attention! The better you use these methods, the more dollars you'll have~ This article has collected many "methods" for making extra money. Come and get them!


Skill 1: Do side jobs, take on outsourcing projects



Beginners in the workplace often feel their salary is not enough, so they take on side jobs. Taking on outsourcing is a fairly discreet matter, and staying anonymous is best! If your direct supervisor finds out, they will only think you have too much free time at work, and it may well increase your workload!


Skill 2: Participate in programming competitions



Compared with doing outsourcing, if your abilities allow and you are confident enough, why not participate in programming competitions held by major organizations or websites? If you are good at algorithms, earning a lot of income is a must!


Skill 3: Build connections and do consulting



This type of side job generally suits programmers with 3-5 years of rich work experience. Under the premise of having certain network resources, you can completely start another "consulting life"! Consulting falls under "experience sharing among technical experts." With this big halo of context, you don't need to stay anonymous. Perhaps your employer will appreciate you even more if they know you are a big name who enjoys sharing experience and technology!


Skill 4: Submit technical articles



Submitting articles (technical干货) to well-known IT development websites can also earn you article fees. A colleague of mine insists on writing one technical insight article per week, and now he always has a full wallet. Starting your own blog is also a great idea!


Skill 5: Record your own teaching videos


Online education has become the direction many entry-level programmers choose for self-study. So, even if you are not good-looking, you don't need to show your face to record teaching videos and make money! When you cultivate an audience habit and get users to keep browsing your technical video tutorials or articles, you both increase your income and accumulate visibility. Why not do it!
