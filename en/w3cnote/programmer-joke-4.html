Programmer humor, you wouldn't understand!

Categories Programmer Jokes

People often say programmers lead dull lives and are rigid people, but that's because you don't understand programmers. Beyond code, these highly intelligent people are humorous and interesting. Forums are often where they showcase their talent, BLOGs are where they share technology, and on BBS and other places, when there are problems, they always help enthusiastically with answers, handling program exceptions, fixing program errors, and so on.
