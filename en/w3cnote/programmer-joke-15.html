Programmer Jokes: Similarities Between Bugs and Mosquitoes

Categories Programmer Life

1

Similarities between bugs and mosquitoes:

1. You don’t know where they’re hiding.

2. You don’t know how many there are.

3. They always show up right when you’re about to go to sleep and rest.

2

A: I’ve been watching One Punch Man lately, and I think we programmers are a bit like Teacher Saitama!

B: How so?

A: The balder, the stronger!

B：……

3

Before National Day, a very cute female colleague suddenly asked me, “Are you free today?”

I was taken aback for a moment, thinking, “Finally a girl with discerning eyes!” So I smiled and answered, “Yes!”

Then… I swapped shifts with her…

4

Both PM and PM2.5 can harm programmers’ health.

It’s just that one is inside the company, the other is outside.

5

A programmer went for an interview. The interviewer asked: “You only graduated two years ago, so where did you get three years of work experience?!” The programmer replied: “Overtime.”

6

Required reading for programmers:

Stage 1:

  • “C Language Programming and Design”
  • “Advanced C++ Handbook”
  • “Swift Introduction and Practice”

Stage 2:

  • “How to Not Get Angry”
  • “Laozi”
  • “Silent Anger”

Stage 3:

  • “Cervical Spondylosis Rehabilitation Guide”
  • “Daily Care for Lumbar Disc Herniation”
  • “Self-Recovery from OCD”

Stage 4:

  • “To Live”

7

A few programmers went out to eat. Someone ordered a dish: spicy bullfrog. Then one of them said he didn’t eat bullfrog, so the person ordering directly drew two slashes in front of “spicy bullfrog,” like this:

// Spicy Bullfrog

No one at the scene thought there was anything wrong with that.

8

When I was a kid, I thought “early to bed, early to rise, makes you healthy” was just a slogan.

But after I honorably became a programmer, I realized these are damn three impossible wishes!

9

Boss: Xiao Cheng, the new version must go live before you get off work!

Xiao Cheng: OK.

The next day, the boss came to work and asked Xiao Cheng: Why isn’t the new version live yet?

Xiao Cheng: I haven’t gotten off work yet.

10

Two programmers. One is highly skilled, rigorous, responsible, with very few bugs, and still single. The other is average, sloppy, with a bunch of bugs, and is often called over by the female tester to receive criticism. Later he became her boyfriend…

11

A programmer friend of mine said he once spent €4,000 on a bag for a girl he had only dated for a week, then broke up with her immediately. I asked him why, why! He said because the girl had set a rule—if you don’t buy me a bag, you don’t love me. He thought that logic was f***ing ridiculous but hard to break. So, in order to defeat her logically, he bought the bag and then dumped her, proving, “I can send you a bag and still not love you.”

12

In the evening, for a classmate’s birthday, we went to sing KTV. A beautiful female engineer from his company insisted on a drinking contest with me. She said if she lost, she’d agree to do one thing for me, and if I lost, I’d have to do the same for her.

As expected, I won, so I couldn’t wait to take her home… hehehe…

After we got home, I looked into her big watery eyes and said with deep affection: “Please help me code, and also help me fix bugs.”

Afterward, I hid alone under the covers and secretly praised myself: I’m really goddamn clever!
