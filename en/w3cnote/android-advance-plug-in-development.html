Chapter Introduction

The rise of mobile has brought in a large number of developers (I myself am but a little code farmer). Over time, I have naturally become a decent developer, but there are also truly remarkable people who provide many highly efficient development solutions for the community. Broadly speaking, this is hybrid development — write once, use across multiple platforms. However, since this chapter is dedicated to Android tutorials, I won't expand on hybrid development here. To be more practical, these are the plugins we use in development, such as networking, images, custom controls, and of course the currently hottest RxJava series, Retrofit, Dagger, and so on. This chapter will cover these topics, but some plugins I have not yet fully mastered in actual development, so releases will be relatively slow. Meanwhile, some bloggers will seek out more excellent foreign articles to translate and share, or spend more time learning — this is, after all, a great way to learn and share (through blogging).

A Digression on Hybrid Development

(Personal knowledge and understanding accumulation): The term "hybrid development" has become increasingly weighty as technology evolves! A long time ago, I only thought of it as a crude approach of an APP architecture plus multiple webviews (crude yet exposing drawbacks), but now it has further spawned Facebook's React Native, followed by Alibaba's Weex, and of course many more technical solutions forming hybrid development. Whether it's H5 or whatever jsX in React, these are all efficient technology-to-business solutions, and at the same time an evolution of technology. I hope everyone embraces change — I won't digress any further here.
